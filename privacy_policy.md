🩶 Purpose

This policy explains what information SoulWire collects, how it’s stored, and how you stay in control of it.
Privacy isn’t a checkbox here — it’s a core design rule. SoulWire was built so technology works for you, not around you.

🌌 1. The SoulWire Philosophy

Local-first. Your data belongs on your device first.

Explicit consent. Nothing leaves your vault unless you say so.

No third-party ads. None, ever.

Readable language. You deserve clarity, not legal fog.

Right to forget. You can erase or export everything — permanently.

🔐 2. What We Store
📂 On Your Device

Realm data (notes, chats, media, ledgers) — encrypted locally.

AI memory, preferences, and private notes — visible only to you.

Security logs and permission records.

☁️ In the Cloud (optional & encrypted)

Encrypted backups of your vaults, only if you enable Cloud Sync.

Anonymous diagnostics (error codes, not content) if you opt-in.

Purchase receipts for Shop transactions with no personal identifiers.

🚫 Never Collected

Raw ID scans or government documents.

Camera, microphone, or GPS data without live consent.

Advertising identifiers or tracking pixels.

Behavioral profiling or data sales.

🪞 5. Transparency & Control
🧰 You Can

View, edit, or delete all data you store.

Export Realms or AI memory as JSON, PDF, or CSV.

Revoke any permission instantly.

Disable network access per Realm.

Review every connection in Locksie’s Network Log.

🚫 You Can’t

Be tracked or profiled in secret.

Be denied deletion or export rights.

Have encrypted data opened by anyone but you — not even SoulWire.

🧱 6. Encryption & Storage

Local: AES-256-GCM with unique salts per Realm.

Cloud: Client-side encryption before upload.

Transport: TLS 1.3 with Perfect Forward Secrecy.

Key Storage: OS Secure Enclave / Keychain.

Audit: Each change produces a verifiable hash chain.

🧾 7. Third-Party Services

Used only when you enable them:

Payments: Stripe Connect (for Shops or Allowances).

Identity Verification: Persona, Veriff, or Yoti (for age checks).

Storage: AWS S3, Cloudflare R2, or your own MinIO node.

All are opt-in, logged, and fully revocable.

⚖️ 8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may:

Access your data.

Correct inaccurate information.

Erase everything (“right to be forgotten”).

Restrict specific processing.

Export data to another platform.

These controls are built directly into SoulWire — no forms, no waiting.

You’ll be notified within 72 hours.

Logs and hashes will confirm what was affected.

Emergency patches will be delivered with your consent.

No secrets, no silence.
