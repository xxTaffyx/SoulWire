🌌 SoulWire Security & Trust Manifesto

“Technology should protect people — not the other way around.”
🕯 Hollow Hearth warmth • 🌌 SoulWire Teal integrity • 🔮 Dream-Circuit transparency

🩶 Our Promise

SoulWire was built on a single principle:

Privacy is a right. Safety is sacred. Trust is earned.

Every Realm — from streaming to schooling, from finances to family — is grounded in ethics, consent, and clarity.
We don’t just encrypt your data; we design every click around your autonomy.

🔐 1. Local-First by Design

All Realms can function offline or on private local networks.

You control your own data vault — nothing uploads automatically.

When syncing is enabled, communication passes through end-to-end encrypted bridges only.

Backups are encrypted client-side before they ever touch a server.

Even in offline mode, you still have full access to your family, health, and creative data.

🧭 2. Age Bands & Realm Gating

SoulWire separates accounts into strict, verified age tiers.
Each tier is enforced by the platform kernel and supervised by the AI guardians.

Age Band	Access Rules	Supervision
CHILD_0-12	No independent accounts. Can only exist under a verified Family Group via guardian invite.	Betsy + May + Lana jointly sandbox data and communications.
TEEN_13-17	Teen Mode: isolated social spaces, restricted visibility, no adult Realms (Creator, Payments, etc.).	Betsy moderates; Tilda tutors; Lana enforces hard boundaries.
ADULT_18+	Full access with opt-in to adult features (streaming, business, finance).	Standard privacy controls; no cross-age visibility by default.

Additional policies:

Automatic Age Cutover: birthdays trigger one-time verified transitions (with notifications and parental sign-off).

Cross-Age Communication: only possible through verified Family Link channels.

No Discovery: teens cannot be searched or messaged by adults outside their verified group.

Media Filters: News, Entertainment, and Gaming realms automatically adjust to age bands.

🧱 3. Explicit Consent, Always

Every Realm asks before sharing, syncing, or showing content.

No silent background uploads or “helpful” auto-sharing.

Consent logs are timestamped and viewable.

“Hidden Mode” immediately disables all outward signals until re-enabled.

Every feature includes a plain-language “Why We Ask” explanation.

🌍 4. End-to-End Encryption Everywhere

AES-256 / ChaCha20 encryption for data at rest and in transit.

Keys generated in the device’s secure enclave; never transmitted or stored centrally.

Passwords are hashed with Argon2; MFA optional but encouraged.

Voice/video calls use ephemeral DTLS-SRTP keys.

Each Realm runs its own encryption context — compromise in one cannot unlock another.

💬 6. Communication & Moderation Integrity

Real-Time Scanning: only metadata patterns, never message content, checked for abuse triggers (violence, grooming, threats).

Zero-Tolerance Enforcement: verified human review only after AI escalation.

Immutable Chat Logs: can’t be deleted or edited — only amended for legal or safety reasons.

“Guardian Witness” Mode: guardians can receive read-only mirrors for minors they’re responsible for.

Offline Cache Purge: users can fully delete local message history at any time.

🧠 7. Human-Readable Security

Every setting uses everyday language (“Who can see this?” instead of “ACL policy”).

Tooltips describe what data moves, where, and for how long.

The entire privacy policy is under 2,000 words and written for comprehension, not obfuscation.

🪙 8. Financial & Data Separation

Payments Realm isolated from Finances Realm to prevent cross-risk.

Stripe-style tokens stored in separate encrypted containers.

No shared cookies, no analytics pixels, no tracking scripts.

Betsy’s financial logs reference transaction hashes, never bank details.

🧩 9. Immutable Integrity

Legal and medical logs use Merkle-tree hash-chains; every amendment is time-stamped.

Co-Parent, Business, and Housing Realms export verifiable proof bundles (ZIP + JSON + PDF receipts).

Chain proofs can be verified offline with our open audit tool soulwire-verify.

🧬 10. Safety in Community & Crisis

“Help Now” Protocol: if triggered, Lana contacts only pre-verified emergency services using stored, consented address data.

Domestic Abuse Mode: instantly duplicates evidence into a hidden encrypted folder outside the visible UI.

Medical Emergency Mode: May can alert chosen caregivers or clinics via encrypted channel.

All emergency events produce immutable audit receipts viewable later.

🧑‍🏫 11. Transparency & Education

Open documentation of all security layers, Realm interactions, and encryption specs.

Optional “Explain It To Me” mode — Tilda teaches the user how their data moves.

Annual third-party security audits published publicly.

All vulnerabilities disclosed responsibly under the SoulWire Security Policy (see SECURITY_DISCLOSURE.md).

🕊️ 12. Dignity by Design

No ads, no dopamine manipulation, no algorithmic ranking.

Interfaces are accessible, readable, and non-exploitative.

Users are never measured by engagement, only connection.

Every Realms’ UI passes WCAG AAA color and contrast compliance.
