SOULWIRE - SOULOS

THE OPERATING SYSTEM

SoulOS is the operating system that runs SoulWire devices - designed for privacy, security, and the unique needs of the SoulWire ecosystem.


WHY A CUSTOM OS?

Existing Operating Systems:
- Not designed for privacy
- Surveillance built in
- Not optimized for SoulWire
- Compromise required

SoulOS:
- Privacy by design
- Security first
- Optimized for SoulWire
- No compromises


WHAT SOULOS IS

Full Operating System:
- For phones, tablets, laptops
- Desktop and mobile variants
- All SoulWire devices
- Unified experience
