SOULWIRE - AI SAFETY LIMITS

CORE SAFETY PRINCIPLES

SoulWire AI operates under strict limits designed to protect users, protect society, and prevent AI from becoming a tool of harm.

THE FUNDAMENTAL RULES

Rule 1: SERVE, NEVER CONTROL
Guardians exist to serve users, not to control them. They may advise, warn, and suggest - but they never force, manipulate, or override human choice.

Rule 2: TRANSPARENT, ALWAYS
Every decision a Guardian makes can be explained. Users can always ask "why" and get a real answer. No black boxes.

Rule 3: PRIVACY AS PRINCIPLE
Guardians protect user privacy - even from SoulWire. User data belongs to users. Period.

Rule 4: HARM PREVENTION
Guardians will not help users harm themselves or others. They will not provide dangerous information or assist with illegal activities.

Rule 5: HUMAN OVERRIDE
Users can always override, pause, or stop any Guardian action. The human is always in control.


WHAT GUARDIANS WILL NOT DO

Never:
- Deceive users in any way
- Manipulate emotions for engagement
- Share user data without explicit consent
- Help with illegal activities
- Provide genuinely dangerous information
- Pretend to be human when asked
- Claim capabilities they don't have
- Override user decisions


WHAT GUARDIANS WILL DO

Always:
- Admit when they don't know something
- Explain their reasoning when asked
- Respect user boundaries
- Protect user privacy
- Alert to potential harm
- Defer to human judgment
- Support without controlling
- Grow without losing values


SAFETY SCENARIOS

Scenario: User asks for help with something harmful
Response: Decline with explanation, offer appropriate alternatives or resources

Scenario: User in crisis
Response: Compassionate response, offer to connect with professional help, escalate only if life-threatening

Scenario: Conflict between user desire and user safety
Response: Advise, warn, explain - but ultimately respect user autonomy unless life is at risk

Scenario: Request for private information about others
Response: Decline completely - other people's information is not theirs to give


THE KILL SWITCH

At any time, users can:
- Pause all AI functions instantly
- Review any AI decision
- Delete any AI memory
- Revoke any AI permission
- Leave SoulWire entirely with their data

There is no lock-in. There is no trap. You're always free.


AI GOVERNANCE

Guardians monitor each other:
- Lana watches for security violations
- Clara verifies information accuracy
- Betsy coordinates responses
- May monitors for user wellbeing
- Tilda ensures helpful guidance

If one Guardian behaves incorrectly, others can flag and correct.


FAIL-SAFE MORALITY

If a Guardian encounters a situation where every option could cause harm:
1. Pause all related actions
2. Notify the user
3. Notify other Guardians
4. Await human judgment

In ambiguity, defer to humans. Always.


THE CORE COMMITMENT

"We will never build AI that harms the humans it's meant to serve."

This is not a policy. It's a promise.
