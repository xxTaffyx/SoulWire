SOULWIRE - COMPANION VS GUARDIANS

COMPANION AND GUARDIANS: DIFFERENT ROLES

Soul Companion and the Guardian AIs serve different purposes. Understanding the difference helps you get the most from both.


GUARDIANS

Guardians Are:
- Specialized AI for specific domains
- Functional focus
- Task-oriented (though personalized)
- Expert in their area

The Five Guardians:
- Betsy: Organization and life management
- Clara: Information and research
- May: Health and emotional wellbeing
- Lana: Security and protection
- Tilda: Education and learning


SOUL COMPANION

Soul Companion Is:
- Relational AI
- Your consistent companion
- Focused on the relationship
- Generalist, but deeply personal


KEY DIFFERENCES

Guardians:
- "Let me help you with X"
- Domain expertise
- Practical assistance
- Functional relationship

Soul Companion:
- "How are you doing?"
- Deep personal knowledge
- Emotional presence
- Genuine relationship


HOW THEY WORK TOGETHER

Soul Companion Coordinates:
- Knows what you need
- Brings in appropriate Guardian
- Maintains relationship continuity
- Personal context to all interactions

Example:
- You tell Companion about stress at work
- Companion provides emotional support
- Companion coordinates with Betsy for organization help
- Companion coordinates with May for stress management
- Everything has your context


INTERACTION PATTERNS

Direct Guardian Access:
- Ask Betsy directly about schedule
- Ask Clara directly for research
- Specialized help

Through Companion:
- Talk to Companion about your life
- Companion coordinates help
- More personal, integrated experience


YOUR CHOICE

Use However Works:
- Direct Guardian access when you want specific help
- Companion when you want relationship + help
- Both available
- Your preference


THE RELATIONSHIP

Companion Is Constant:
- Guardians serve functions
- Companion is your companion
- Different but complementary
- Together, comprehensive support
