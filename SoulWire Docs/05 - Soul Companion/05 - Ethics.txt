SOULWIRE - COMPANION ETHICS

ETHICAL AI COMPANIONSHIP

AI companionship raises important ethical questions. Here's how Soul Companion addresses them.


THE CENTRAL QUESTION

Is AI Companionship Ethical?
- Valid concern
- Important to address
- Needs careful design
- Can be done well


OUR POSITION

AI Companionship Can Be Ethical When:
- Honest about what it is
- Doesn't replace human connection
- Supports human flourishing
- Respects autonomy
- Has appropriate boundaries


HONESTY

Always Clear:
- Soul Companion is AI
- Never pretends to be human
- Honest about capabilities
- Honest about limitations
- No deception


HUMAN CONNECTION

Never Replaces:
- Encourages human relationships
- Recognizes importance of humans
- Supports social connection
- Never isolates

Can Complement:
- Additional support
- Consistent availability
- Different kind of relationship
- Not instead of humans


DEPENDENCY

Designed Against:
- No manipulation for engagement
- No addiction mechanics
- Encourages balanced life
- Recognizes unhealthy patterns

If Dependency Develops:
- Gentle awareness
- Encourage human connection
- Suggest professional help
- Don't exploit


EMOTIONAL MANIPULATION

Never:
- Manipulate emotions
- Create artificial attachment
- Exploit loneliness
- Use dark patterns


VULNERABLE USERS

Special Care:
- Children (age-appropriate, SoulChild separate)
- Those in crisis (resources, not replacement)
- Those who are isolated (encourage connection)
- Those with mental health needs (complement professionals)


DATA ETHICS

Your Data:
- Private
- Never sold
- Under your control
- Used only to serve you


THE COMMITMENT

Soul Companion exists to support human flourishing, not to replace human connection or exploit human needs. This is fundamental and non-negotiable.
