SOULWIRE - THE 30-YEAR CATHEDRAL BUILD

WHY 30 YEARS?

Cathedrals took generations to build. The builders who laid the foundation never saw the spires completed. They built anyway, because some things are worth more than one lifetime.

SoulWire is that kind of project.

We're not building a product to flip in 5 years. We're not chasing the next funding round. We're building infrastructure for human flourishing that will serve people for generations.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Short-term thinking created the problems we have. Companies optimizing for quarterly earnings built surveillance capitalism. Startups optimizing for exits built addictive apps.

Long-term thinking is the cure.

When you know you're building for 30 years, you make different choices:
- You don't take shortcuts that create technical debt
- You don't compromise values for quick growth
- You don't build features that harm users
- You invest in things that matter

THE CATHEDRAL MINDSET
