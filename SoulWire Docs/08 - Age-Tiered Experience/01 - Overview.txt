SOULWIRE - AGE TIER OVERVIEW

APPROPRIATE FOR EVERYONE

SoulWire provides age-appropriate experiences for everyone - from children to seniors. The same ecosystem, adapted to life stage.


THE CONCEPT

One SoulWire, Many Experiences:
- Same core system
- Adapted to age
- Appropriate features
- Appropriate restrictions
- Grows with you


WHY AGE TIERS?

Because:
- A 5-year-old and 50-year-old have different needs
- Content appropriate for adults isn't for children
- Seniors need different accessibility
- Life stages have different requirements

SoulWire adapts rather than being one-size-fits-all.


THE TIERS

Children (SoulChild):
- 0-17 years
- Parental oversight
- Age-appropriate content
- Safety first
- Growing capability

Adults:
- 18+ years
- Full features
- Full autonomy
- Full responsibility

Seniors (SoulElder):
- Optional adaptations
- Accessibility focus
- Simplified options available
- Family coordination features


HOW IT WORKS

Age Verification:
- Birth date required
- Verification for age-restricted features
- Cannot be falsified easily
- Appropriate access granted

Automatic Adaptation:
- Features adjust to age
- Content filtering adjusts
- Interface can adjust
- Guardians adjust communication style


TRANSITIONS

Smooth Transitions:
- SoulChild to Adult at 18
- Gradual capability increases
- No jarring changes
- Continuous experience


WITHIN TIERS

Further Adaptation:
- Children: Multiple sub-tiers (5 vs. 15 are very different)
- Adults: Customizable to preferences
- Seniors: Choose level of adaptation


THE GOAL

Everyone gets SoulWire that works for them. Children are protected. Adults have full capability. Seniors have accessibility. No one is excluded.
