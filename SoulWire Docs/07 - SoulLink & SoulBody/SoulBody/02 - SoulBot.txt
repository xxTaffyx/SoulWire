SOULWIRE - SOULBOT HOME ROBOT

PRESENCE IN YOUR HOME

SoulBot is a home robot - mobile, helpful, and a physical embodiment of your Guardian AIs.


WHAT SOULBOT IS

Home Robot:
- Mobile (moves through home)
- Screen face (expressive)
- Camera and sensors
- Speaker and microphone
- Robotic arm options (advanced models)


CAPABILITIES

Presence:
- Move to you when you call
- Be physically present
- Face-to-face interaction
- More engaging than speaker

Check On Things:
- Patrol mode (check the house)
- See what's happening
- Remote viewing
- Did I leave the stove on?

Communication:
- Video calls on the move
- Follow conversation
- Be your presence when remote

Basic Tasks (Advanced Model):
- Fetch small items
- Basic manipulation
- Interaction with environment


GUARDIAN EMBODIMENT

Your Guardians, Physically:
- Betsy can "come find you" for reminders
- Clara can show you things on screen
- May can check on you physically
- Physical relationship, not just voice


USE CASES

Elderly Care:
- Check-ins
- Companionship
- Medication reminders
- Fall detection/alert

Remote Work:
- Be present at home while working
- Check on kids
- Check on pets
- Receive packages

Security:
- Home patrol
- Alert investigation
- Physical presence deterrent


MODELS

SoulBot:
- Basic mobility
- Screen and sensors
- Voice interaction
- Most users

SoulBot Pro:
- Advanced mobility
- Manipulation arm
- More sensors
- Advanced users


SAFETY

Designed Safe:
- Can't hurt people
- Obstacle avoidance
- Careful movement
- Emergency stop


PRIVACY

Your Robot, Your Control:
- Camera can be disabled
- Privacy modes
- Not recording everything
- You control
