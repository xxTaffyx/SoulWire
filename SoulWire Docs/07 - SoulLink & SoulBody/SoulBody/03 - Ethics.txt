SOULWIRE - SOULBODY ETHICS

ETHICAL PHYSICAL AI

Physical AI embodiment raises significant ethical questions. SoulWire's approach to responsible development.


THE QUESTIONS

Physical AI Raises:
- Safety in physical space
- Replacement of human workers
- Relationship implications
- Accessibility vs. isolation
- Care quality concerns


SAFETY FOUNDATION

Physical Safety First:
- Cannot harm humans (Asimov-like principles)
- Extensive safety testing
- Fail-safe designs
- Careful, slow deployment

Testing:
- Rigorous safety testing
- Real-world validation
- Continuous monitoring
- Quick response to issues


LABOR IMPLICATIONS

Honest About This:
- Robots will affect jobs
- We must address this
- Support for transitions
- Not pretending otherwise

Our Position:
- Robots should help, not just replace
- Job transition support
- New job creation focus
- Economic impact consideration


CARE ETHICS

For Elder Care/Health Care:
- Supplements human care
- Does not replace human connection
- Enables human care to be better
- Not warehousing with robots

Standards:
- Human oversight required
- Regular human contact required
- Not isolation enabler
- Dignity preservation


RELATIONSHIP ETHICS

Physical AI Companions:
- Same ethics as digital companions
- Not replacement for humans
- Healthy relationship boundaries
- Honest about what they are


CONSENT

In Spaces:
- Privacy in others' spaces
- Recording consent
- Presence consent
- Respect for preferences


DEVELOPMENT APPROACH

Slow and Careful:
- Not rushing to market
- Safety over speed
- Ethics over profit
- Long-term thinking


THE COMMITMENT

Physical AI must be developed responsibly. The stakes are higher in physical space. We commit to prioritizing safety, ethics, and human wellbeing over speed or profit.
