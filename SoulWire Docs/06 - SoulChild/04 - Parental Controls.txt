SOULWIRE - SOULCHILD PARENTAL CONTROLS

PARENTS IN CONTROL

SoulChild gives parents real control over their children's technology use - not fake controls that kids bypass in minutes.


CONTROL PHILOSOPHY

Real Control:
- Actually enforced
- Can't be easily bypassed
- Parent determines level
- Appropriate to child's age

Not Surveillance:
- Oversight, not spying
- Trust building
- Age-appropriate privacy
- Preparing for independence


WHAT PARENTS CAN CONTROL

Screen Time:
- Daily limits
- Per-app limits
- Schedule (no devices during dinner)
- Bedtime enforcement
- Flexible when needed

Content:
- Age tier setting
- Additional restrictions
- Specific content blocking
- Approval for new content

Communication:
- Who can contact child
- Approval for new contacts
- Visibility into communications
- Messaging features enabled/disabled

Features:
- Which features are available
- When features are available
- Purchase restrictions
- App installation approval


VISIBILITY

Parents Can See:
- Screen time usage
- Apps used
- Communications (age-dependent)
- Location (if enabled)
- Concerning activity alerts

Balance:
- Visibility decreases with age
- Building toward independence
- Trust development
- Privacy respected


CONTROLS BY AGE

Younger Children:
- Full parental control
- Complete visibility
- All approvals required
- Very limited autonomy

Middle:
- Significant control
- Moderate visibility
- Some independent decisions
- Growing autonomy

Older Teens:
- Reduced control
- Limited visibility
- Most decisions independent
- Preparing for adulthood


MULTIPLE PARENTS

Co-Parenting:
- Both parents can have control
- Shared settings
- Coordinated approach
- Works for separated families


IMPLEMENTATION

How It Works:
- Parent account linked to child account
- Settings controlled via parent interface
- Enforcement at system level
- Cannot be bypassed by child


CONVERSATIONS

Encouraging Discussion:
- Controls as conversation starters
- Why limits exist
- Collaborative setting (when appropriate)
- Building digital citizenship
