SOULWIRE - SOULCHILD SAFETY

PROTECTING CHILDREN

Safety is the foundation of SoulChild. Every feature is designed with child safety as the primary consideration.


CONTENT SAFETY

Age-Appropriate Content:
- All content rated and filtered
- Inappropriate content blocked
- AI-assisted content moderation
- Human review for edge cases

What's Filtered:
- Violence (age-inappropriate)
- Sexual content
- Hate content
- Dangerous information
- Predatory content


COMMUNICATION SAFETY

Protected Communication:
- Family contacts always allowed
- Friend contacts require parent approval
- No stranger contact possible
- All communication can be reviewed

Monitoring:
- Parents can see communications
- AI flags concerning patterns
- Human review when needed
- Privacy balanced with safety


ONLINE SAFETY

Internet Protection:
- Safe search enforced
- Known dangerous sites blocked
- Real-time content filtering
- Age-appropriate results only

Social Media:
- No external social media until appropriate age
- SoulChild social features are safe
- Moderated environments
- Parent visibility


PREDATOR PROTECTION

Lana Protects:
- Grooming pattern detection
- Suspicious behavior flagging
- Stranger contact prevention
- Immediate alerts to parents

Zero Tolerance:
- Any predatory behavior = immediate action
- Law enforcement cooperation
- Child safety above all


DATA PROTECTION

Children's Privacy:
- Extra protection for children's data
- COPPA compliance (and beyond)
- No advertising to children
- No data selling ever
- Minimal data collection


SCREEN TIME

Healthy Use:
- Screen time limits available
- Usage tracking
- Break reminders
- Balance encouragement

Not Addiction:
- No addiction mechanics
- No infinite scroll
- No engagement optimization
- Designed for healthy use


PARENT ALERTS

When Parents Are Notified:
- Concerning communication
- Attempted policy bypass
- Safety issues
- Unusual patterns


SAFETY EDUCATION

Teaching Kids:
- Online safety education
- Tilda teaches safe practices
- Age-appropriate awareness
- Empowering kids to protect themselves
