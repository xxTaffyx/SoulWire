SOULWIRE - SOULBAND

FITNESS FOCUS

SoulBand is a fitness tracker for those who want dedicated workout and activity tracking without full smartwatch complexity.


FOR

People Who Want:
- Fitness tracking focus
- Simple interface
- Long battery life
- Affordable entry point
- Activity motivation

Activity:

- Distance

- Active minutes
- Flights climbed

Workouts:
- Multiple sport modes
- Workout detection
- Heart rate zones
- Performance metrics

Health:
- Heart rate
- Sleep tracking
- Stress monitoring

Simple Band:
