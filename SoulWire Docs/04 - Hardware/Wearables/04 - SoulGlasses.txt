SOULWIRE - SOULGLASSES

AUGMENTED, NOT REPLACED

SoulGlasses are AR (augmented reality) glasses that enhance your world without replacing it - information when you need it, invisible when you don't.


DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Augmented Reality Done Right:
- Enhance the real world
- Don't replace human interaction
- Information at a glance
- Disappear when not needed

Not:
- Isolation device
- Social barrier
- Always-on distraction
- Creepy recording machine


WHAT THEY DO

Heads-Up Information:
- Navigation directions
- Incoming call/message notification
- Calendar reminders
- Quick information display

Contextual Help:
- Translation overlays
- Name reminders (consent-based)
- Meeting information
- Helpful context
