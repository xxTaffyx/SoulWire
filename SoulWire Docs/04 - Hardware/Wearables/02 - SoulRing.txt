SOULWIRE - SOULRING

HEALTH, INVISIBLY TRACKED

SoulRing is a smart ring that tracks health metrics without the screen, notifications, or distraction of a smartwatch.


THE CONCEPT

For Those Who:
- Don't want a smartwatch
- Prefer invisible tech
- Want health data without notifications
- Like traditional watches
- Want 24/7 health tracking


WHAT IT TRACKS

Health Metrics:
- Heart rate (continuous)
- Heart rate variability
- Sleep quality
- Blood oxygen
- Temperature trends
- Activity/steps

Wellness Indicators:
- Readiness score
- Recovery status
- Stress indicators
- Cycle tracking (optional)
