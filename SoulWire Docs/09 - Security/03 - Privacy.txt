PRIVACY PHILOSOPHY

Your Data Belongs to You:
- Not to us
- Not to advertisers
- Not to governments
- To you, only you

We Are Stewards:
- We hold your data
- We protect your data
- We serve your interests
- We never exploit your data


WHAT WE DON'T DO

Never:
- Sell your data
- Sell access to you
- Share for advertising
- Mine for profit
- Exploit your information

This Is Absolute:
- Not "except when..."
- Not "unless..."
- Never means never


WHAT WE COLLECT

Only What's Needed:
- To provide the service
- To improve for you
- With your knowledge
- Minimized always

Transparent:
- You can see what we have
- You can understand why
- You can delete it
- You can export it


DATA CONTROL

You Control:
- What data is collected
- How it's used
- Who sees it
- When it's deleted

- Consent management

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
