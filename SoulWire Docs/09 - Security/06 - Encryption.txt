SOULWIRE - ENCRYPTION

ENCRYPTION EVERYWHERE

How SoulWire uses encryption to protect your data - in transit, at rest, and end-to-end.


ENCRYPTION PHILOSOPHY

Everything Encrypted:
- Data moving: encrypted
- Data stored: encrypted
- Communications: encrypted
- Maximize protection

Defense in Depth:
- Multiple layers
- If one fails, others protect
- Belt and suspenders approach


IN-TRANSIT ENCRYPTION

Data Moving:
- TLS 1.3 everywhere
- Certificate pinning
- No downgrade attacks
- Modern cipher suites

Protects Against:
- Man-in-the-middle attacks
- Network eavesdropping
- WiFi snooping


AT-REST ENCRYPTION

Data Stored:
- All data encrypted at rest
- Strong encryption algorithms
- Key management best practices
- Even we can't access without keys

Protects Against:
- Data breaches
- Physical theft
- Insider threats


END-TO-END ENCRYPTION

For Communications:
- Messages encrypted end-to-end
- Only you and recipient can read
- We cannot read your messages
- Even if compelled

How It Works:
- Keys on your devices
- We don't have the keys
- Cannot be decrypted in transit
- True privacy


ZERO-KNOWLEDGE (WHERE POSSIBLE)

Some Data:
- Encrypted so we can't see it
- Only you can decrypt
- True privacy
- Trade-off: We can't help if you lose key

Available For:
- Sensitive vaults
- Private notes
- Certain storage

- Recovery planning

- Protecting against future threats
- Staying ahead
