SOULWIRE - COMMUNITY & CONDUCT

COMMUNITY STANDARDS

How we expect people to behave in SoulWire spaces - and how we behave ourselves.


COMMUNITY PRINCIPLES

Respect:
- Treat everyone with dignity
- Different views welcome
- Personal attacks not
- Harassment never

Inclusion:
- Everyone welcome
- Diversity valued
- Accessibility prioritized
- No discrimination

Honesty:
- Be truthful
- Be authentic
- Don't deceive
- Don't manipulate

Safety:
- Physical safety
- Emotional safety
- Safe spaces maintained
- Protection from harm


USER CONDUCT

Expected:
- Respectful communication
- Legal behavior
- Community benefit
- Good faith participation

Not Allowed:
- Harassment
- Hate speech
- Illegal activity
- Exploitation
- Spam
- Abuse of systems


ENFORCEMENT

Approach:
- Education first when appropriate
- Clear warnings
- Escalating consequences
- Permanent ban for serious violations

Process:
- Fair review
- Appeal available
- Consistent application
- Transparent policies


CONTENT STANDARDS

Allowed:
- Legal content
- Respectful expression
- Diverse viewpoints
- Creative expression

Not Allowed:
- Illegal content
- Exploitation content
- Harassment
- Hate content
- Dangerous content


MODERATION

How We Moderate:
- AI-assisted detection
- Human review
- Community reporting
- Consistent standards

Transparency:
- Clear about what's moderated
- Appeal processes
- Moderation reports
- Policy explanations


OUR CONDUCT

SoulWire Commits To:
- Treating users with respect
- Being honest in communication
- Responding to concerns
- Continuous improvement


COMMUNITY BUILDING

What We Want:
- Healthy community
- Constructive engagement
- Mutual support
- Positive environment

How We Build:
- Encourage positive behavior
- Support community leaders
- Provide good tools
- Model good behavior
