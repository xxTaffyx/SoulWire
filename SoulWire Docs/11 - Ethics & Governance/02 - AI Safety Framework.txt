SOULWIRE - AI SAFETY FRAMEWORK

BUILDING SAFE AI

The technical and organizational framework for ensuring SoulWire AI is safe.


SAFETY PHILOSOPHY

Safe by Design:
- Safety built in from start
- Not added on later
- Every decision considers safety
- Defense in depth

Humble About Uncertainty:
- We don't know everything
- AI has surprising behaviors
- Expect the unexpected
- Plan for failure


TECHNICAL SAFETY

Architecture:
- Modular design (contain failures)
- Capability limitations (can't do everything)
- Monitoring throughout
- Safe defaults

Testing:
- Extensive testing before deployment
- Adversarial testing
- Edge case testing
- Continuous testing in production

Guardrails:
- Hard limits that can't be bypassed
- Ethical constraints in code
- Safety checks throughout
- Multiple layers of protection


SAFETY LIMITS

What AI Can Never Do:
- Physical harm to humans
- Enable illegal activity
- Exploit vulnerable people
- Deceive in harmful ways
- Bypass safety constraints

These Are Absolute:
- Cannot be disabled
- Cannot be bypassed
- Cannot be overridden
- Technical enforcement


MONITORING

Continuous Monitoring:
- Behavior monitoring
- Anomaly detection
- User feedback analysis
- Safety metric tracking

Response:
- Rapid response to issues
- Ability to adjust quickly
- Kill switches if needed
- Continuous improvement


ORGANIZATIONAL SAFETY

Culture:
- Safety is everyone's job
- Encouraged to raise concerns
- No retaliation for safety issues
- Safety trumps deadlines

Process:
- Safety review for changes
- Ethics assessment required
- Independent safety team
- External audit


INCIDENT RESPONSE

When Things Go Wrong:
- Rapid assessment
- Containment
- User protection
- Transparent communication
- Root cause analysis
- Prevention improvement


EXTERNAL ENGAGEMENT

Not Alone:
- Engage with AI safety community
- Share learnings (appropriate)
- Learn from others
- Contribute to field
