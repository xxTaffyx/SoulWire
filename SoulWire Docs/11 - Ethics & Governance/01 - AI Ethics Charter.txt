SOULWIRE - AI ETHICS CHARTER

THE ETHICAL FOUNDATION

SoulWire's AI Ethics Charter - the fundamental principles that guide all AI development and deployment.


CORE PRINCIPLES

1. Human Flourishing
AI exists to help humans flourish - not to replace human agency, not to exploit, not to manipulate, but to genuinely help people live better lives.

2. Safety First
AI safety is not optional. We will not deploy AI that poses unacceptable risks. Better to go slower than to cause harm.

3. Transparency
We are honest about what our AI is, what it can do, what it can't do, and what we know and don't know about its behavior.

4. Privacy
AI will protect human privacy, not violate it. Personal data is not training fodder. Surveillance is not a feature.

5. Consent
Humans remain in control. AI should empower human choice, not subvert it. Manipulation is forbidden.

6. Honesty
AI will not deceive. It will be clear about its nature, its limitations, and its uncertainties.

7. Non-Maleficence
AI will not be used to harm. We will not build weapons. We will not enable oppression. We will not participate in harm.

8. Accountability
We are accountable for what our AI does. "The algorithm did it" is not an excuse. Humans are responsible.


APPLICATION

These Principles Apply To:
- All AI development
- All AI deployment
- All decisions about AI
- All SoulWire activities

They Are:
- Non-negotiable
- Not subject to business pressure
- Reviewed and updated thoughtfully
- Enforced at all levels


GOVERNANCE

Who Enforces:
- AI Ethics Board
- Independent oversight
- Internal champions
- External audit

How:
- Regular review
- Ethics assessment for new features
- Ongoing monitoring
- Response to concerns


THE COMMITMENT

This is not marketing. These principles guide actual decisions. When there's a conflict between ethics and profit, ethics wins. That's the commitment.
