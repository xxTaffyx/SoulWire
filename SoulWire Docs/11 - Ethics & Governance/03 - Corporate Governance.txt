SOULWIRE - CORPORATE GOVERNANCE

BUILT DIFFERENT

SoulWire's corporate structure is designed to protect the mission - not optimized for maximum shareholder value, but for long-term mission fulfillment.


THE PROBLEM

Normal Companies:
- Legally required to maximize shareholder value
- Short-term pressure
- Ethics sacrificed for profit
- Mission drift inevitable

We Saw:
- Google: "Don't be evil" -> removed
- Facebook: "Connect people" -> surveillance capitalism
- Everyone: Mission erodes under pressure


THE STRUCTURE

Public Benefit Corporation:
- Legal structure that allows mission priority
- Not required to maximize shareholder profit
- Can consider all stakeholders
- Mission protected in charter

Dual-Class Stock (If Public):
- Mission-aligned control maintained
- Prevents hostile takeover
- Long-term thinking enabled
- Not subject to activist pressure


MISSION LOCK

Charter Provisions:
- Mission cannot be changed easily
- Ethics charter is binding
- Supermajority required for changes
- Protected from erosion


GOVERNANCE

Board:
- Independent directors
- Mission-aligned members
- AI ethics expertise
- User representation

Committees:
- AI Ethics Committee
- Safety Committee
- Privacy Committee
- Not just Audit and Compensation


LEADERSHIP

Requirements:
- Mission alignment
- Long-term thinking
- Ethics commitment
- Not just business credentials


SUCCESSION

Planning:
- Mission continuity prioritized
- Values passed forward
- Not just financial succession
- Generational thinking


STAKEHOLDER PRIORITY

When Conflicts:
1. User safety and wellbeing
2. Ethical principles
3. Employee welfare
4. Long-term sustainability
5. Financial returns

This Order Matters:
- Profit doesn't come first
- Users come first
- Ethics don't bend for revenue


TRANSPARENCY

Reporting:
- Mission progress reports
- Ethics compliance reports
- Safety reports
- Beyond financial reporting
