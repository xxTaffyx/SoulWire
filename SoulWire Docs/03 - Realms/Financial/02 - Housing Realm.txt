SOULWIRE - HOUSING REALM

YOUR HOME, ORGANIZED

The Housing Realm manages everything related to where you live - whether you rent, own, or are looking for a new place.


CORE FEATURES

For Renters:
- Lease tracking and management
- Rent payment scheduling
- Move-in/move-out documentation
- Landlord communication records
- Security deposit tracking
- Lease renewal reminders

For Homeowners:
- Mortgage tracking
- Property tax management
- Home value monitoring
- Equity calculation
- Refinance analysis

For House Hunters:
- Search across all platforms (no more checking 10 sites)
- Saved searches and alerts
- Viewing scheduler
- Comparison tools
- Neighborhood research (via Clara)


MAINTENANCE & UPKEEP

Maintenance Tracking:
- When things were last serviced
- Upcoming maintenance needs
- Appliance warranties and manuals
- Contractor contact management
- Home improvement projects

Betsy helps with:
- "Your HVAC filter is due for replacement."
- "The roof was last inspected 3 years ago - might want to schedule that."
- "Warranty on your dishwasher expires next month."


DOCUMENTATION

The Housing Realm keeps:
- Lease agreements
- Mortgage documents
- Home inspection reports
- Repair receipts
- Insurance policies
- Property photos (move-in condition)
- Appliance manuals


MOVING SUPPORT

When you move, the Housing Realm helps:
- Moving checklist
- Address change management
- Utility transfer coordination
- Move-in inspection documentation
- Unpacking task management


ROOMMATES & SHARED HOUSING

For shared living situations:
- Shared expense tracking
- Chore scheduling
- House rules documentation
- Move-out settlements


INTEGRATION

With SoulBank: Rent/mortgage payments, budgeting for housing
With Family Realm: Family home coordination
With Home Realm: Smart home device integration
