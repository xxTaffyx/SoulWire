SOULWIRE - VEHICLE REALM

YOUR VEHICLES, MANAGED

The Vehicle Realm handles everything about your cars, motorcycles, boats, or any other vehicles you own or use.


CORE FEATURES

Vehicle Management:
- Registration tracking
- Insurance management
- Title documentation
- Loan/lease tracking

Maintenance:
- Service history
- Upcoming maintenance schedules
- Oil change reminders
- Tire rotation tracking
- Recall alerts
- Warranty tracking


MAINTENANCE INTELLIGENCE

Betsy alerts you:
- "Oil change due in 500 miles"
- "Registration expires next month"
- "There's a recall on your vehicle - here are the details"
- "Your tires have about 10,000 miles left based on your driving"

Clara researches:
- Best mechanics in your area
- Fair prices for services
- Common issues with your make/model
- Recall information


FUEL & COSTS

Tracking:
- Fuel expenses
- Maintenance costs
- Insurance costs
- Total cost of ownership
- Fuel efficiency trends

Analysis:
- "Your car costs about $X per month to operate"
- "Fuel efficiency dropped this month - might want to check tire pressure"


BUYING & SELLING

When Shopping:
- Research assistance (Clara)
- Price comparison
- Vehicle history reports
- Dealership reviews
- Financing options

When Selling:
- Market value estimates
- Listing assistance
- Documentation preparation


MULTIPLE VEHICLES

For families or those with multiple vehicles:
- All vehicles in one place
- Per-vehicle tracking
- Family vehicle scheduling
- Shared vehicle coordination


INTEGRATION

With SoulBank: Vehicle payments, insurance, fuel expenses
With Family Realm: Family vehicle sharing
With Travel Realm: Road trip planning
With SoulDrive: In-vehicle integration
