SOULWIRE - CHARITY REALM

GIVING WITH IMPACT

The Charity Realm helps you give effectively - making sure your generosity actually helps and tracking your charitable activity over time.


CORE FEATURES

Charity Research:
- Organization verification
- Financial transparency ratings
- Impact assessments
- Red flag identification
- Comparison tools

Clara helps verify:
- Is this charity legitimate?
- How much goes to actual programs vs. overhead?
- What's their actual impact?
- Are there any concerns?


DONATION MANAGEMENT

Tracking:
- All donations in one place
- Tax-deductible vs. non-deductible
- Recurring donations
- Annual giving summary
- Impact reports from organizations

Tax Support:
- Automatic categorization
- Tax document collection
- Donation receipt storage
- Annual tax summary


GIVING STRATEGY

For Intentional Givers:
- Annual giving budget
- Cause allocation (e.g., 40% education, 30% environment, 30% local)
- Goal tracking
- Impact measurement

Betsy helps:
- "You've given $X toward your $Y giving goal this year"
- "That recurring donation to [org] - still want to continue?"


CAUSE DISCOVERY

Finding Where to Give:
- Cause exploration
- Local vs. global options
- Emergency/disaster giving
- Effective giving recommendations

Clara researches:
- Most effective organizations for [cause]
- Local organizations making a difference
- Current emergency needs


PROTECTION

Scam Prevention:
- Charity verification before giving
- Scam alerts
- Fake charity identification
- Secure giving channels

Lana monitors:
- "That charity email looks suspicious - let me verify."
- "This organization isn't registered as a charity."


IMPACT TRACKING

Over Time:
- Total lifetime giving
- Causes supported
- Organizations helped
- Estimated impact


INTEGRATION

With SoulBank: Donation payments
With Memory Realm: Your giving history
With Family Realm: Family giving coordination
