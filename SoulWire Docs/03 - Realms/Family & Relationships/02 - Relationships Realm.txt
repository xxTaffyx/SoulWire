SOULWIRE - RELATIONSHIPS REALM

CONNECTION, NURTURED

The Relationships Realm helps you maintain and nurture all your personal relationships - not just family, but friends, extended network, and meaningful connections.


CORE FEATURES

Contact Management:
- All relationships in one place
- Relationship context (how you met, shared history)
- Communication preferences
- Important dates

Betsy helps remember:

- "Your college roommate's birthday is tomorrow."
- "You mentioned wanting to catch up with [person]."
