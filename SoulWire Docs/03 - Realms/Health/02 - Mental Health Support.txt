SOULWIRE - MENTAL HEALTH SUPPORT

SUPPORT, NOT REPLACEMENT

SoulWire provides mental health support features - resources, tools, and AI companionship - but never replaces professional mental health care.


MAY'S ROLE

May Provides:
- Emotional check-ins
- Active listening
- Resource recommendations
- Crisis resource information
- Non-judgmental presence

May Does Not:
- Diagnose conditions
- Replace therapy
- Prescribe treatment
- Provide medical advice
- Substitute for professionals


SUPPORT TOOLS

Daily Tools:
- Mood tracking
- Journaling (private, never shared)
- Gratitude practices
- Breathing exercises
- Grounding techniques

Wellness Resources:
- Meditation library
- Relaxation exercises
- Sleep support
- Stress management
- Mindfulness practices


CRISIS SUPPORT

When You're Struggling:
- Crisis hotline information
- Emergency contacts
- Safety planning tools
- Check-in features

May recognizes distress and provides resources:
- "I'm concerned about what you're sharing. Here are some resources that might help."
- "Would you like me to help you connect with professional support?"
- "You don't have to go through this alone."

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US)

PRIVACY

Mental Health Data:
- Most protected data in SoulWire
- Never shared without explicit consent
- Never used for advertising
- Never sold
- Encrypted and protected


BOUNDARIES

What AI Can Do:
- Listen without judgment
- Provide comfort
- Suggest resources
- Track patterns
- Be consistently available

What AI Cannot Do:
- Replace human connection
- Provide therapy
- Diagnose conditions
- Prescribe treatment
- Be your only support


THE GOAL

Support your mental health journey by providing tools and resources while encouraging professional care when needed. Be helpful, never harmful, and know our limits.
