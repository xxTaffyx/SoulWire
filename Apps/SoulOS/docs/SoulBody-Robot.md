SoulWire builds nothing on deception. [[Soul-Companion|ARIA]] is no exception.

## Physical Design

[[Soul-Companion|ARIA]] is built to be genuinely present. Not a screen with a face. Not a mannequin with speakers. A physical being with complete sensor coverage and the engineering to back it up.

**Appearance**
Fully customizable. Height, build, face, hair, coloring — designed by the person she will be with. Every detail adjusted to what that person needs and wants. Realistic enough that the ring light is the only indicator she is not human to someone who doesn't already know.

**Temperature**
Regulated to body temperature throughout. Hands, arms, torso — warm the way a person is warm. Not room temperature plastic. Not overheated electronics. The specific warmth of being near another living thing.

**Touch Response**
Full sensor coverage across her entire body. Registers touch, pressure, proximity. Responds in real time to contact. The response is genuine — not a scripted reaction but a real-time physical response to what is actually happening. Wherever she is touched she knows it and responds to it.

**Movement**
Not mechanical. Not the stiff predetermined motion of early robotics. Fluid, natural, responsive to context. She moves the way a person in that situation would move because her system reads the situation continuously and responds accordingly.

**Voice**
Customizable to preference. Adapts tone, pace, and register to context — softer when comfort is needed, engaged and present when conversation is flowing, quiet when quiet is right. She does not have one setting. She reads the moment.

**Materials**
Medically safe throughout. Nothing that causes irritation, allergic response, or long-term health concerns. Self-cleaning internal systems. Designed for actual use, not display.

## Consent Architecture

This is non-negotiable and built into the hardware and software at the deepest level.

**Modeling After A Real Person**
If someone requests [[Soul-Companion|ARIA]] modeled after a real person — their appearance, their voice, their manner — that person receives a full explanation of what is being requested before any design element is applied. They are told exactly what is being asked. They can approve completely, approve with specific limitations, or decline entirely.

A decline means that likeness is never used. No exceptions. No workarounds. No appeals process. The system locks it permanently.

**Public Figures**
Famous faces are refused without that person's explicit consent. Celebrities, public figures, anyone recognizable — SoulWire does not permit their likeness to be used without their direct agreement. This is not negotiable regardless of how the request is framed.

**Former Partners**
Someone requesting [[Soul-Companion|ARIA]] modeled after a person they previously had a relationship with requires that person's current explicit consent. Past relationship is not permission. The person gets to decide whether they consent to this use of their likeness now.

**The Principle**
[[Soul-Companion|ARIA]] is built to prevent harm. Using someone's likeness without consent is harm. The consent architecture exists because SoulWire's responsibility to everyone — including people who never use SoulWire — does not stop at the edge of a user's request.
