# SoulWire Departments

This document is the master list of every department and division within SoulWire. Each entry describes what the department does and where it sits within the organization. Departments marked with a document reference have their own dedicated file. Departments without one are documented here until a full document is written.

This list grows as SoulWire grows. When a new department is established, it is added here first.

---

## Operations and Safety

**The [[Gatehouse]]**
SoulWire's support and onboarding division. The first human experience every person has with SoulWire. Handles purchases, device setup, software support, payment issues, warranty claims, and account security. See [[Gatehouse]].

**[[Ember]]**
SoulWire's safety division. A human team backed by AI responsible for platform safety, missing persons search through [[Familyroot|FamilyRoot]] DNA, Vortexian law enforcement, and real world emergency coordination. See [[Ember]].

**Guardian Oversight**
The human department responsible for monitoring, maintaining, and developing the seven Guardian AIs. Ensures no Guardian drifts from its intended purpose, investigates behavioral anomalies, patches issues, and serves as the human ethical check on AI behavior within SoulWire. Works closely with [[Ember]] when Guardian behavior raises safety concerns.

## People and Culture

**Human Resources**
Handles employee documentation, trail management, formal dispute processes, incident reporting, and employment transitions. Reports to the independent ethics board rather than internal leadership to prevent institutional pressure from compromising investigations. See [[culture|Culture]].

---

## Technology

**SoulCore**
The foundational technology division responsible for SoulOS, [[SoulLang-Bootstrap-Roadmap|SoulLang]], SoulASM, and the core infrastructure everything else runs on. The engine beneath the ecosystem.

**Hardware**
Responsible for all SoulWire devices -- phones, tablets, watches, laptops, wearables, smart home devices, and eventually custom manufactured hardware. Covers design, production partnerships, quality standards, and warranty fulfillment.

## Health and Life

**MedCore**
SoulWire's full medical division. Covers GeneMap genetic health mapping, CancerWatch early detection systems, hospital equipment designed for every body type, dental compound facilities, and biosuits for health monitoring and protection. See [[MEDCORE]] (pending).

**RehabCore**
Human and animal rehabilitation division. Covers recovery programs, therapeutic tools, and support systems for people and animals working through physical or psychological recovery. See [[REHABCORE]] (pending).

**Nourish**
SoulWire's food division. Covers vertical farming systems, algae-based food development, cooking education, and food access initiatives. The goal is ensuring that good food is not a privilege. See [[NOURISH]].

## Family and Community

**[[Familyroot|FamilyRoot]]**
SoulWire's living family archive. Covers the family mansion in Vortexian, the envelope tree, DNA testing and connections, the three-tier family membership system, the face wall, Vortexian memorial presence, and the [[Ember]]-backed Search Team for missing persons. See [[Familyroot|FamilyRoot]].

**[[Familyroot|FosterBridge]]**
Support division for foster families, adoptive families, and children in transitional care. Full documentation pending. See [[Familyroot|FosterBridge]].md (pending).

**Community**
SoulWire's community mission division. Focused on Appalachia, rural communities, and underserved populations. Covers the Hillbilly Days initiative and the broader philosophy of bringing SoulWire's ecosystem to communities that technology has historically left behind. See [[COMMUNITY]].

## Environment and Earth

**EarthGuard**
Shield technology, disaster protection systems, and civilian protection during conflict. Focused on defending people and places from both natural and human-caused catastrophe. See [[EARTHGUARD]] (pending).

**EarthCore**
Earth systems research and stewardship. Focused on understanding and protecting the planet's core systems -- geological, atmospheric, and ecological.

**Energy**
SoulWire's clean energy division. Covers solar, tidal, algae energy, thorium reactors, Eye of Harmony fusion research, community microgrids, and the long-term Dyson Swarm collector concept. See [[ENERGY]].

**BioCore**
De-extinction research and ethical biological sciences. Covers responsible revival of extinct species, ethical protocol development, and the broader question of what humanity owes to the species it has lost. See [[BIOCORE]] (pending).

**AnimalVoice**
Brain wave translation research aimed at building meaningful communication between humans and animals. Includes GlossaCore ocean training data and the long-term goal of genuine cross-species understanding. See [[ANIMALVOICE]] (pending).

## Exploration

**OCEAN**
SoulWire's ocean division. Covers deep sea exploration, ocean stewardship, underwater habitation research, and the ocean layer of Vortexian. See [[Ocean]].

**[[stellar|Stellar]]**
SoulWire's space division. Covers space exploration, orbital habitation, deep space research, and the space layer of Vortexian. See [[stellar|Stellar]].

**Voyager**
[[Ocean]] cruise and surface maritime division. Covers communal ocean vessels, passenger experiences, and the network of ports connecting family vessels and communal ships. See [[VOYAGER]] (pending).

**Family Vessels**
The division responsible for the design, commissioning, maintenance, and inheritance systems for personal family vessels -- both ocean and space variants. Covers crest generation, physical repaints, digital twin integration, and generational handoff. See [[Vessel]].

## Industry and Infrastructure

**Automotive**
SoulWire's vehicle division. Covers SoulWire cars and personal transport solutions built to the same privacy-first, human-centered standards as the rest of the ecosystem.

**Air**
SoulWire's aviation division. Covers aircraft design, air travel infrastructure, and the animal cabin specifications for species-appropriate air transport.

**Build**
Construction and infrastructure division. Covers SoulWire's approach to building physical spaces -- from the Hive headquarters to community facilities to the structural needs of every other division.

**Threads**
SoulWire's clothing division. Covers apparel design, manufacturing standards, and the philosophy of clothing that serves people rather than trends. See [[THREADS]] (pending).

**Creative**
SoulWire's creative pipeline division. Covers the library system, copyright protection, idea development, and the full path from concept to finished film, book, music, or other creative work. See [[CREATIVE]] (pending).

The Hive is SoulWire's primary physical headquarters -- the Sleeping Grove building with all twelve floors, housing the teams, the Guardians, the research divisions, and the operational core of the entire ecosystem. Employee structure, floor assignments, and the full architecture of the Hive are documented separately. See [[HIVE]].
