## 🧠 THE KEY INSIGHT: NEURAL VS PHYSICAL

There are TWO ways to feel someone:

### Method 1: Physical Haptics (Devices Touch You)

Devices physically create pressure/sensation on your body. 

| Pro | Con |
|-----|-----|
| No surgery | Requires wearing devices |
| Tangible | Limited to where devices are |
| Current technology | Can look awkward in public |

### Method 2: Neural Presence (Brain Thinks It's Real)

SoulLink sends signals to your brain — you FEEL touch without anything physical.

| Pro | Con |
|-----|-----|
| No physical device needed | Requires SoulLink (Band, Flex, or Core) |
| Works anywhere | More advanced tech |
| Invisible to others | Band is non-invasive; Flex/Core are invasive |
| Full-body sensation possible | |

**This is the key for public use — with SoulLink Band, you can feel a hug while walking down the street and no one knows.**

## 👫 HUMAN-TO-HUMAN PRESENCE (REVISED)

### The Problem With Physical Haptics

Old approach:
> "Partner hugs themselves in SoulVest → You feel it in your SoulVest"

**Problem:** Hugging yourself in public looks weird.  

### The New Approach: Intent-Based Transmission

You don't have to perform the action — you THINK or TRIGGER it.

| Method | How It Works | Best For |
|--------|--------------|----------|
| **Thought trigger** | Think "send hug" → Partner feels hug | SoulLink users |
| **App trigger** | Tap button → Partner feels sensation | Non-SoulLink users |
| **Gesture trigger** | Subtle hand gesture → Sends feeling | SoulGrip/Ring users |
| **Voice trigger** | "Send [partner] a hug" → They feel it | Anyone |
| **Scheduled** | Pre-set comfort at certain times | Automatic |

### How Each Device Handles Sending

| Device | How You SEND | How Partner RECEIVES |
|--------|--------------|---------------------|
| **SoulPhone** | Tap "send hug" button | Whatever devices they have |
| **SoulWatch** | Double-tap crown | Haptic pulse on their watch |
| **SoulRing** | Squeeze ring twice | Their ring squeezes back |
| **SoulGrip** | Squeeze gesture | Pressure on their hands |
| **SoulVest** | App button (don't hug yourself) | Pressure on their vest |
| **SoulLink Band** | Think "send hug" | Neural hug sensation |
| **SoulLink Flex/Core** | Think the sensation | Full sensation received |

### Receiving Without Awkwardness

| Situation | Best Receive Method |
|-----------|---------------------|
| **In public** | SoulLink Band (invisible) or SoulWatch (subtle pulse) |
| **At home** | Full haptics (SoulVest) for immersive feel |
| **At work** | SoulRing squeeze or SoulWatch pulse |
| **Sleeping** | SoulVest for full comfort presence |
