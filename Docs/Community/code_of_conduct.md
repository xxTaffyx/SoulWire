🕯️ SoulWire Code of Conduct

“We build a world worth being in — not just logging into.”
🌌 SoulWire Teal trust • 🕯 Hollow Hearth safety • 🔮 Dream-Circuit empathy

Last updated: November 11, 2025

🩶 Purpose

This document defines how every person inside SoulWire — users, developers, parents, teachers, healers, and creators — must behave toward one another.

SoulWire is not a platform of noise or status; it’s a shared home.
Respect is not optional here — it’s the first rule of entry.

🌌 1. Universal Expectations

Be kind, even when correcting.

Respect boundaries. No harassment, sexualization, or manipulation.

No hate speech, slurs, or discrimination of any kind.

No unsolicited DMs or spam.

No NSFW or explicit content outside clearly marked adult spaces (18+ verified).

Consent is sacred — in speech, sharing, or collaboration.

Keep private things private. Never repost DMs, logs, or medical/family data.

No impersonation of others or the SoulWire brand.

No exploitation, scams, or false offers.

Safety reports come first. Anyone may contact
📧 xxtaffyx@gmail.com
 or
💬 SoulWire Support Server

to report abuse, bugs, or dangerous content.

Breaking these rules may result in warnings, suspension, or permanent bans depending on severity.
Lana (Security AI) monitors community interactions for safety triggers — always with transparency.

🔮 2. Age Policy
Group	Access	Notes
Children (<13)	No direct accounts	Use through verified guardian profiles only.
Teens (13–17)	Protected Realms	Must remain in teen or family zones.
Adults (18+)	Full access	May not enter teen spaces.

False age claims lead to permanent account lock.

🧭 3. Realm-Specific Rule Index

Each Realm has its own tone and expectations in addition to the universal rules.

1️⃣ Creator Realm

🎥 Streamers, editors, artists.

No sharing copyrighted or NSFW content without proof of rights.

Credit collaborators.

Respect audience boundaries.

No AI voice or likeness cloning without consent.

2️⃣ Family Realm

🏠 Household management and shared calendars.

All members must be verified and consent to link.

No snooping or editing another person’s entries.

Children cannot remove guardians.

Maintain respectful task language (no humiliation or threats).

3️⃣ Co-Parent Realm

⚖️ Custody, agreements, immutable logs.

No editing or deleting records.

Only amendments allowed, visible to both guardians.

Keep tone factual — not emotional warfare.

Abuse of the system for stalking or shaming = instant removal.

4️⃣ Payments Realm

💰 Allowances, budgets, ledgers.

No fake invoices or chargeback threats.

Must show accurate transaction history.

Do not share banking info publicly.

All disputes go through the dispute form, not public channels.

5️⃣ Education Realm

📚 Students, tutors, schools.

No cheating, plagiarism, or answer-dumping.

Teachers must verify their identity.

No bullying or mocking academic performance.

All lessons must meet safety and age-appropriate standards.

6️⃣ Medical Realm

🩺 Health files and records.

No posting or diagnosing others publicly.

May (AI) handles sensitive data only with consent.

False medical claims or miracle cures are banned.

Respect privacy of all patient records.

7️⃣ Social Realm

💬 Forums and feeds.

No spam, clout-bait, or trolling.

Keep debates civil and fact-based.

No mass-tagging or harassment.

Respect content warnings.

8️⃣ Gaming Realm

🎮 Servers, mods, events.

No cheating, griefing, or harassment in multiplayer spaces.

Respect modders and credits.

NSFW mods banned in public listings.

All giveaways or events must disclose odds and prizes.

9️⃣ Business Realm

🏢 Startups, hiring, transparency.

All job posts must be real and currently hiring.

No MLM, crypto, or pyramid schemes.

Companies must display employment and pay transparency.

Reviews must be factual, not defamatory.

🔟 Entertainment Realm

📺 Movies, shows, and music.

Content must have legal viewing rights.

No illegal streaming or piracy.

Parents must approve teen view lists.

Keep discussions spoiler-tagged and respectful.

1️⃣1️⃣ Task & Project Realm

📋 Productivity tools.

No assigning tasks without consent.

Shared boards require explicit member approval.

Keep project communication polite.

No over-tracking or performance shaming.

1️⃣2️⃣ Home Realm

🧹 Budgets, groceries, repairs.

Be considerate in shared expense lists.

No naming/shaming messy roommates.

Accurate budgets only — no fake data for allowances.

Respect personal schedules.

1️⃣3️⃣ Relationship Realm

💞 Couples & emotional wellbeing.

Mutual consent required for all shared data.

No coercion or passive-aggressive “rules.”

AI May monitors for abuse signals (users may disable).

Relationship logs are private, not shareable.

1️⃣4️⃣ Career Growth Realm

🚀 Jobs, mentorship, and resumes.

No falsifying skills or experience.

Mentors must disclose conflicts of interest.

No solicitation of personal relationships.

Constructive feedback only.

1️⃣5️⃣ Memory Realm

📔 Journals, scrapbooks, and life logs.

Private by default.

No reposting others’ memories without consent.

Do not screenshot emotional posts for gossip.

Respect grief and mental-health content.

1️⃣6️⃣ Safe Browser Realm

🧭 Protected internet portal.

No bypassing filters.

No tracking scripts or external ads.

All outbound links verified by Clara.

Parents may set Safe Mode tiers; tampering = ban.

1️⃣7️⃣ Assistive Tech Realm

🦾 Accessibility hub.

No mocking speech devices or disabilities.

Respect adaptive tool users.

Do not exploit accessibility data for testing.

Contribute improvements, not criticism.

1️⃣8️⃣ Travel Realm

🗺 Routes, budgets, itineraries.

No sharing real-time locations of others.

Review honestly; no fake promotions.

Help stranded travelers respectfully.

No harassment in travel meetups.

1️⃣9️⃣ Creative World Realm

🎨 Writing, music, and art tools.

Credit all creators.

No plagiarism or AI-art theft.

No adult content in teen galleries.

Keep criticism constructive.

2️⃣0️⃣ Elder Care Realm

🌿 Senior support and caregivers.

Respect elders’ autonomy and consent.

No impersonating caregivers or family.

Report neglect or abuse immediately.

Medical info stays private between elder, May, and chosen caregivers.

2️⃣1️⃣ Doctor & Provider Realm

🏥 Verified professionals.

Must show valid license or practice ID.

One free consult for low-income users per policy.

No upselling or false treatments.

Reviews must focus on care quality, not personal details.

2️⃣2️⃣ Identity Realm

🪞 Pronouns, culture, and privacy settings.

Respect chosen names and pronouns.

No forced outing or labeling.

Hate toward any identity = instant ban.

Lana locks offenders permanently.

2️⃣3️⃣ Housing & Renting Realm

🏘 Landlords, leases, and reviews.

No fake listings.

Tenants and landlords must disclose issues honestly.

No discrimination in housing offers.

Scams = permanent ban and report to authorities.

2️⃣4️⃣ Pets Realm

🐾 Care, records, and pet services.

No animal harm or sale for abuse.

Sitters, kennels, and vets must show credentials.

Reviews must be factual, not vindictive.

No exotic pet trades without verified legal proof.

2️⃣5️⃣ Finances Realm

💳 Budgets, debt plans, goals.

No false debt relief offers.

No selling or sharing financial info.

Report phishing or scams immediately.

Financial coaches must prove credentials.

2️⃣6️⃣ Vehicle Realm

🚗 Maintenance & safety.

No promoting illegal mods or street racing.

Mechanics must list real certifications.

Share advice safely — no life-risk shortcuts.

Dashcam footage requires consent before upload.

2️⃣7️⃣ Career & Business Plugins (Meta)

🧩 Third-party plugin creators.

Must disclose every API used.

No data harvesting or ads.

Plugins must pass security and ethical testing.

Violations = blacklisting across the entire network.

⚖️ 4. Enforcement
Level	Description	Result
Notice	Gentle reminder of rules.	Warning & guidance.
Moderation	Behavior pattern issue.	Mute, suspension, or restriction.
Severe	Hate, threats, or harm.	Permanent ban & report.

Appeals may be sent privately to xxtaffyx@gmail.com
 for review.

🕊️ 5. Closing Statement

“A code of conduct isn’t about control — it’s about care.”

SoulWire thrives when everyone inside it feels safe to create, learn, rest, and exist.
Follow the rules not because you must, but because this is a shared home — and we keep it warm together.
