# The Gatehouse

The Gatehouse is SoulWire's support and onboarding division. It is the first human experience every person has with SoulWire -- before the ecosystem opens, before the devices are set up, before Betsy learns your name, there is a Gatehouse agent on the other end of a call making sure you arrive properly. They are not a call center in the traditional sense. They are the people who hold the door open and make sure it closes safely behind you.

Every interaction that involves access, purchase, setup, repair, or account security passes through The Gatehouse. They are the entry point and the safety layer simultaneously.

---

## What The Gatehouse Handles

The Gatehouse manages every point of contact between SoulWire and its users that requires a human being. This includes new device purchases and onboarding, software errors and technical support, payment issues and billing disputes, warranty claims and device replacements, and account security concerns. No matter the reason for the call, the experience is the same -- patient, unhurried, and focused entirely on resolution.

---

## Onboarding

When a new user receives their SoulWire device they are given a choice before setup begins. They can call The Gatehouse and have an agent walk them through the entire setup process, entering basic information on their behalf and ensuring everything is configured correctly before the call ends. Or they can set it up themselves using SoulWire's guided self-setup process.

Neither option is presented as better or more appropriate than the other. The choice exists because different people have different needs and both deserve equal respect. Elders who find technology intimidating should never have to navigate setup alone if they do not want to. Experienced users who want to touch everything themselves should never be slowed down by unnecessary hand-holding. The Gatehouse serves both equally well.

## Account Security and Verification

The Gatehouse operates under absolute verification standards. Before any account information is discussed, any assistance is provided, or any action is taken, the identity of the caller must be confirmed. The account owner receives full access. A designated emergency contact receives limited access under specific documented circumstances. Someone granted verbal permission by the owner -- meaning the owner is present on the call and explicitly approves assistance in real time -- may be helped for that call only. That permission does not carry forward to future calls and is not stored as ongoing authorization.

Anyone outside these categories receives nothing. Not confirmation that the account exists. Not shipping information. Not callback times. Nothing. This line is held warmly but without flexibility, because the people who push hardest against it are often exactly the people it was designed to stop.

## Remote Access Protocol

Gatehouse agents have the ability to access a user's device remotely to diagnose and resolve issues that cannot be solved through instruction alone. This capability exists to serve users and is treated with the seriousness that level of access requires.

No agent can activate remote access alone. A formal request must be submitted and approved by a second authorized person before any session begins. The approval is logged with the requesting agent's ID, the approving party's ID, the customer's verbal consent recorded on the call, the timestamp, and the documented reason for access. The session itself is recorded in full. A closing log entry documents everything accessed and every change made.

When the session ends the user receives an automatic notification summarizing what was accessed, what was changed, and who authorized it. This notification is not optional and cannot be suppressed. The customer is part of the audit system by design.

Agents whose remote access patterns trigger anomalies are automatically flagged by Ember for human review before any action is taken. Suspicious patterns escalate up the chain from Ember investigator to department leadership to executive level to independent ethics board depending on severity. No single person in that chain has the ability to quietly close an investigation. The flag exists in the record above anyone's authority to erase it.

## Error Documentation

Every known error has a corresponding document written in plain language that explains what the error is, why it happens, and exactly how to resolve it. Agents are never expected to guess or improvise when a documented solution exists. If a caller presents an issue that does not have a corresponding document, the agent escalates it immediately and a document is created before that issue is closed. Every edge case makes every future agent more capable. The knowledge base only grows.

---

## Call Standards

There are no time metrics, no call volume scores, and no performance dashboards measuring speed. The only measure of a successful call is whether the problem was resolved and whether the caller felt heard. Agents are trusted to manage their calls with good judgment. Most issues should be resolved within an hour. If a resolution requires follow-up, a callback is scheduled at the customer's preferred time, prioritizing morning hours. When no callbacks are scheduled, that time belongs to the agent.

Customers are treated with patience and respect. Agents are treated the same way. Yelling, abuse, or disrespect from a caller is not something agents are expected to absorb. A caller who cannot engage respectfully will be offered a rescheduled call when they are ready to do so. The standard applies to both sides of every conversation.

---

## Training

All Gatehouse agents complete two months of training before taking their first call. Training covers every error document in the system, the full verification and security protocol, remote access procedures and their ethical weight, the escalation chain and when to use it, how to read and understand their own employment trail, and the values and philosophy behind every policy they will apply. Agents are not trained to memorize scripts. They are trained to understand why things work the way they do so they can exercise good judgment when reality does not match the manual.

---

## Video and Customer Facing Standards

For calls that involve video, marketing appearances, or any customer facing visual context, agents dress to business casual standard at minimum. For standard calls the standard is simple -- come in clean, groomed, and comfortable. What an agent wears has no bearing on their trail or their standing. Tattoos, personal style, and individual expression are entirely their own.
