# SoulWire Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 13, 2026

SoulWire is being designed around privacy as a core system rule, not as a marketing claim. This document describes the intended privacy direction for the ecosystem as it exists now and as future services are developed.

## Core Principles

- local-first where possible
- explicit consent before sharing
- no advertising surveillance
- readable explanations instead of legal fog
- user control over export and deletion

## Categories of Data SoulWire May Handle

Depending on the app, AI, world, or system involved, SoulWire may handle:

- account and profile information
- app content such as notes, messages, media, schedules, records, and settings
- AI interaction history and user-approved memory
- security logs, permissions, and audit information
- optional sync, backup, and diagnostic data

## Design Direction

SoulWire is intended to follow these rules wherever technically possible:

- keep personal data on the user device first
- encrypt sensitive data in transit and at rest
- minimize collection
- separate optional cloud features from core local features
- allow users to see, export, and delete their information

## What SoulWire Is Not Intended To Do

SoulWire is not intended to:

- sell personal data
- use ad trackers or hidden profiling systems
- collect microphone, camera, location, or device data without user action and clear notice
- silently share private conversations with third parties

## AI Privacy Direction

SoulWire's AI systems are intended to operate under strict privacy expectations:

- AI access should be scoped to the task and permissions involved
- AI memory should be visible, manageable, and revocable by the user
- sensitive categories should receive stronger protections
- cross-system handoffs should be explicit where feasible

## Children, Teens, and Safety

SoulWire plans to use age-aware experiences and stronger boundaries for minors. Future implementations may include guardian-managed access, protected spaces, and limited feature availability by age tier.

## Third-Party Services

If third-party providers are used in the future for storage, identity verification, payments, analytics, or infrastructure, SoulWire policy is that they should be:

- limited to what is necessary
- disclosed clearly
- revocable where feasible
- chosen with privacy and safety in mind

## Security and Breach Response

If SoulWire operates hosted systems in the future, the project aims to provide timely notice, public clarity where appropriate, and direct user guidance if a verified security incident affects user data.

## Your Expected Rights

Where applicable and where systems support it, users should be able to:

- access their data
- correct their data
- export their data
- delete their data
- revoke permissions

## Policy Status

This is a living policy for the evolving SoulWire ecosystem. As specific products move from concept to active service, this policy should be revised to reflect the actual technical behavior of those services.
