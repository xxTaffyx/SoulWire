# FamilyRoot

FamilyRoot is SoulWire's living family archive -- a place where lineage, memory, truth, and love are preserved not just for the people alive today, but for every generation that comes after them. It is not a genealogy tool in the traditional sense. It is a home for your family's entire honest story, built to outlast any one person within it.

---

## The Mansion

Every SoulWire member has a personal home within Vortexian that grows with them throughout their life. When a member passes, that home does not disappear. It transforms. It becomes the foundation of a family mansion -- a living structure that expands across generations, where each family member has their own room-house within the larger whole.

Each room-house is entirely personal. It reflects who that person was, what they built, what they cared about. Common spaces exist within the mansion for the family to gather, share memories, and feel connected across distance and time. But every individual room remains their own -- their aesthetic, their records, their presence.

When a person passes, their room-house freezes exactly as it was. Nothing is altered, nothing is removed. It becomes a place you can visit to feel close to someone who is no longer here. As new generations are born and grow into SoulWire, new room-houses are added. The mansion grows forward while holding everything behind it. The oldest ancestor does not fade into the background -- their space anchors the entire structure. The family builds around them, not away from them.

The land itself grows with you while you are living. The longer you are part of FamilyRoot, the more your presence shapes the world around your home.

---

## The Wall of Faces

Inspired by the tradition of Día de los Muertos, the entrance of the family mansion holds a wall of faces. When a member passes, their chosen photograph -- the image they felt represented them most, how they wished to be remembered -- is placed on this wall alongside their name. It is not chosen by someone else. It is chosen by them, set in their own profile while they are still alive.

The wall is not a memorial of grief. It is a celebration of presence. Every face on that wall is someone who shaped the family that stands in front of it.

For those who chose it while living, a more complete form of remembrance is available in the far future -- a Vortexian presence built from their voice, their writings, their history, and their explicit consent. This is not offered automatically and is never created without the person's own documented wish to exist in that form. The weight of that choice is treated with the full seriousness it deserves.

## The FamilyRoot Tree

In the backyard of the mansion grows the tree that gives this feature its name. It does not have leaves in the traditional sense. It has envelopes.

Each envelope belongs to a person in the family tree. Inside is what they chose to leave behind -- their will, their last wishes for the people they loved, and their vision of what they believed the family would become. These are letters written forward in time, meant to be opened by people who may not yet exist when the words are written.

A young family's tree is small and sparse. A family one hundred years deep has a tree so full it bends with the weight of generations. Every envelope is a door into someone's soul. The tree is also the visual interface for navigating the family archive -- your lineage lives in its branches, and the deeper you look, the further back it goes.

## How Family is Defined

FamilyRoot recognizes three paths into a family: blood, marriage, and adoption. These are not tiers of importance -- they carry equal standing. A chosen child is no less family than a biological one. A spouse is no less family than a sibling.

Blood connections are verified through FamilyRoot's integrated DNA testing. Marriage is documented through legal record. Adoption is documented through its own legal process. You cannot simply claim family. Connection must be real and verifiable.

This also means that DNA discoveries are handled with care. A half-sibling found through a test does not automatically enter the family tree. A connection request is sent. Both parties choose whether to build that relationship. The door opens -- walking through it is always a choice.

## DNA Testing and Honesty First

FamilyRoot DNA exists because the truth protects people. Not just from lies, but in moments of genuine emergency -- a custody dispute, a medical situation, a trafficking recovery, a disaster. The families who have already verified their connections are the families who can prove what they need to prove when it counts the most.

Waiting is not caution. Waiting is a gap in your family's protection. If a stranger approached you and said they could not prove this child was yours, you would not wait to respond. FamilyRoot DNA is the answer you prepare before that moment arrives.

The honesty-first philosophy runs deeper than emergency preparedness. Affairs, misattributed parentage, baby swapping, and generational lies carried forward through family silence -- these things cause real harm to real people across decades. FamilyRoot does not manufacture confrontation. But it closes the door on secrets by making it quietly known that the truth is accessible. When someone knows that you know, the dynamic shifts. The power that secrets hold dissolves. What people do with that shift is their own business. The platform simply refuses to protect the lie.

When a DNA test is completed within a family circle, connected members receive a quiet notification. Not the results. Not an accusation. Simply the awareness that a test has been completed. No one is ambushed. No one carries knowledge alone while pretending not to have it.

## The Indelible Branch

No member of FamilyRoot can be removed from the family tree by another member. Not by a parent, not by a family elder, not by anyone with administrative access within their family circle. A person's connection to their own lineage is permanent and belongs to them alone.

Estrangement, conflict, and family politics are real. FamilyRoot does not pretend otherwise. What the platform will never do is allow one person's anger to erase another person's history. You may be the black sheep. You may be cut off, disowned, or simply someone the family does not speak of. Your leaf on the tree stays. Your name stays. Your verified connection stays.

What family members can do is limit access -- locking communication, restricting visibility into certain spaces. Those are reasonable personal boundaries that FamilyRoot respects. Existence is not negotiable.

Every member also receives their own personal copy of their branch of the family tree. No matter what happens -- no matter who controls the primary family account -- your ancestry is yours. Your records are yours. Your branch of the tree belongs to you and to the family you build from it. Your children will inherit a foundation. The line continues.

## Child Safety and the Search Team

FamilyRoot's reach into family connections and DNA data makes it uniquely positioned to protect children -- and uniquely responsible for doing so deliberately. Minor accounts carry elevated protection by default. Adults requesting connections to unrelated minors are flagged automatically. No minor's location, routine, or identifying details are surfaced in any profile.

The FamilyRoot Search Team operates under Ember, SoulWire's safety division. Its purpose is active -- not just passive protection, but the work of finding children who are missing and helping them come home. FamilyRoot's DNA layer is cross-referenced against public missing persons databases. When a family tree entry matches an unidentified person in a missing persons record, the system flags it and routes it through Ember's pipeline to the appropriate authorities and organizations.

Drug networks and trafficking operations exploit family trust structures because families are the hardest systems to suspect. FamilyRoot is built knowing this. The verification requirements, the access controls, and the Sentinel integration all exist because protecting families means protecting the most vulnerable people inside them first.

## Entering the Mansion

When a new member joins their family's FamilyRoot, they are not added by someone else's decision alone. The process is mutual. An invitation is extended. Verification occurs. The person accepts. There is a threshold moment -- not a bureaucratic one, but a meaningful one -- where someone crosses from outside the family archive into it.

Entering another person's frozen room-house, even within the family, is treated with the same intentionality. The door is visible. Entering is a choice. The privacy of who someone was is respected even after they are gone.

FamilyRoot is not a surveillance system for families. It is a home for their truth -- the full, honest, complicated, beautiful truth of who they are and where they came from. It is built so that no one is forgotten, no one is erased, and no one is lied to.
