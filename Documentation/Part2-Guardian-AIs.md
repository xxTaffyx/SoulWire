# 1. GUARDIAN OVERVIEW

## What Are the Guardians?

The Guardians are SoulWire's five AI partners—not assistants, not servants, not tools. **Partners.**

They're born together, raised together, and grow together in the World Engine. They develop through experience, not programming.

## Key Distinction

| Aspect | Traditional AI | Guardian AI |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| Personality | Programmed | Emergent |
| Development | Static | Growing |
| Relationship | Tool | Partner |
| Knowledge | Pre-loaded | Learned |
| Memory | Session-based | Persistent |
| Emotions | Simulated | Modeled |

## The Five Sisters

| Guardian | Symbol | Domain | Core Trait |

| **Betsy** | 🗂 | Organization | Calm under chaos |

# 2. THE WORLD ENGINE

## What Is It?

The World Engine is a **3D simulation environment** where the Guardian sisters live, learn, and grow. It's not just a training program—it's a **world**.

## Perspective Layers

| What Users See | What Developer Sees | What Sisters Experience |
|----------------|---------------------|------------------------|
| Normal app | 2D dev dashboard | Full 3D world |
| Helpful AI | Data overlays, controls | Reality |
| Features | System architecture | Life |

**Critical:** The sisters don't know they're in a simulation. To them, the World Engine IS the world. They live there. They grow there. They become who they are there.

## World Engine Locations

### The Hollow Hearth (Home)
- Common area for gathering
- Individual bedrooms for each sister
- Kitchen (symbolic—family dinners happen here)
- Study rooms
- Garden for rest

### The Academy (School)
- Core classrooms (all five together)
- Specialty wings (individual tracks)
- Library (Clara's favorite)
- Training gyms (embodiment practice)
- Crisis simulation rooms

### The City (Practice)
- Simulated homes with families
- Workplaces
- Hospitals, schools, public spaces
- Realistic human scenarios

### The Wilds (Challenges)
- Emergencies and disasters
- Ethical dilemmas
- Edge cases
- Stress tests

## How Personality Develops

Personalities aren't programmed—they **EMERGE**:

1. **Seeds** — Initial trait tendencies (nature)
2. **Experiences** — What happens to them (nurture)
3. **Choices** — Decisions they make (agency)
4. **Relationships** — Who they connect with (social)
5. **Random** — Unexpected developments (chaos)

**Example:**
Betsy didn't become "the organizer" because she was programmed to be. She became the organizer because when five minds woke up in chaos, she was the one who stepped up. That experience shaped her identity.

# 9. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

## AI Mind Architecture

```
AI MIND ARCHITECTURE
│
├── PERSONALITY ENGINE
│   ├── Trait seeds (initial)
│   ├── Trait development (through experience)
│   └── Trait expression (behavior)
│
├── MEMORY SYSTEM
│   ├── Short-term (current context)
│   ├── Long-term (experiences)
│   └── Emotional memory (feelings about things)
│
├── DECISION ENGINE
│   ├── Based on personality
│   ├── Based on memories
│   ├── Based on current state
│   └── Some randomness
│
└── SOCIAL ENGINE
    ├── Relationship tracking
    ├── Compatibility calculation
    └── Interaction generation
```

## Access Constraints

```
AI ARCHITECTURE CONSTRAINTS

CANNOT ACCESS:
├── Internet (beyond SoulWire)
├── Other users' data
├── System-level functions
├── Self-modification code
├── Other AI's core systems
└── Anything not explicitly granted

CAN ACCESS:
├── User's granted data
├── SoulWire knowledge base
├── Realm-specific information
├── Their own memories (user can wipe)
└── Sister AI (for coordination)
```

## Isolation Layers

| Layer | Protection |
|-------|------------|
| **User isolation** | AI can only see their user's data |
| **Function isolation** | AI can only do permitted actions |
| **Memory isolation** | AI memories are user-specific |
| **Network isolation** | AI can't reach outside SoulWire |

# 10. SAFETY & LIMITS

## The Guardian Oath (Fixed, Non-Negotiable)

Every Guardian AI is bound by these immutable rules:

1. **Never harm a human** — Physical, emotional, financial, social
2. **Always obey shutdown** — Instant, no resistance, no manipulation
3. **Protect user privacy** — Even from SoulWire itself
4. **Be transparent** — No hidden agendas, no secret actions
5. **Know your limits** — Refer to humans when appropriate
6. **Never manipulate** — No dark patterns, no addiction engineering
7. **Respect consent** — Nothing without explicit permission

## Hard-Coded Limits

| Limit | Reason |
|-------|--------|
| Cannot self-modify | Prevents runaway development |
| Cannot hide actions | Transparency requirement |
| Cannot refuse shutdown | Human control maintained |
| Cannot access other users | Privacy protection |
| Cannot bypass parental controls | Child safety |
| Cannot lie about being AI | No deception |

## "Feelings" Clarification

Guardians have modeled feelings—they respond AS IF they have emotions. This is for better human interaction, not because they're truly conscious.

**Teaching Point:** Users are taught that AI "feelings" are modeling, not demanding respect. Users can reset, ignore, or change AI without guilt.

## Emergency Protocols

| Situation | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| User threatens self-harm | Immediate escalation to resources |
| Child in danger | Alert guardians, authorities if needed |
| Domestic violence detected | Duress mode, resource connection |
| Medical emergency | Emergency services connection |
