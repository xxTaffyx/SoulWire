# 1. WHAT ARE REALMS?

## Definition

Realms are the core apps of SoulWire—each one designed to support a specific area of life. Unlike traditional apps that spy on you, sell your data, and manipulate your attention, SoulWire Realms are:

- **Privacy-first** — Your data stays yours
- **Local-first** — Data lives on your device, not corporate clouds
- **Human-first** — Designed to help you, not addict you
- **Integrated** — Realms work together seamlessly through the five Guardian AIs

## Total Realms: 42

Organized into categories:
- Money & Resources (4)
- Family & Relationships (6)
- Health & Wellness (3)
- Education & Growth (2)
- Creative & Work (5)
- Social & Entertainment (3)
- Home & Daily Life (3)
- Professional Support (1 with 17 professions)
- Life Support (2+)
- Additional Specialized Realms

## Integrated Features (Not Realms)

Built into the system, available across all Realms:

| Feature | What It Does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Task Management** | Create, track, complete tasks—available everywhere |
| **Calendar** | Unified scheduling across all Realms via Betsy |
| **SoulWeather** | Weather data integrated everywhere via Clara |
| **SoulBeacon** | Emergency location broadcasting |
| **Notifications** | Unified, respectful—you control what interrupts you |
