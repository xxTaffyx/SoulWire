Last updated: April 13, 2026

This policy explains how the SoulWire name, ecosystem names, AI identities, and brand elements may and may not be used.

All SoulWire trademarks, brand identifiers, and related trade dress are claimed by xxTaffyx.

This policy applies to the SoulWire ecosystem, including but not limited to:

- `SoulWire`

- `SoulLang`

- `SoulOS`
- the seven Guardian identities: `Betsy`, `Clara`, `May`, `Lana`, `Tilda`, `ARIA`, and `Alex`
- app names, world names, realm names, and system names used in the repository
- logos, icons, wordmarks, slogans, taglines, and naming systems

## What You May Do

Without separate written permission, you may:

- refer to SoulWire factually in commentary, research, education, reviews, or news coverage
- link to the official repository or official SoulWire pages
- use phrases such as `for SoulWire`, `compatible with SoulWire`, or `integration for SoulWire` in a truthful descriptive way
- show limited screenshots for commentary, review, or educational discussion

These uses must be accurate and must not imply endorsement, partnership, employment, sponsorship, or official status.

## What Requires Permission

You need written permission before you:

- use SoulWire branding in a product, service, company, studio, event, or commercial offering
- register a domain, social handle, or package name that uses SoulWire or protected SoulWire names
- publish merchandise using SoulWire or Guardian branding
- create a hosted service, public server, fork, or derivative platform using SoulWire branding
- reuse or closely imitate SoulWire visual identity in a way that suggests official affiliation

## Prohibited Uses

The following are not allowed:

- pretending to be the official SoulWire project or team
- using Guardian names or identities as your own AI product or character brand
- releasing `official`, `pro`, `lite`, `redux`, `plus`, or similar branded variants of SoulWire without permission
- creating deceptive clones that rely on SoulWire naming, lore, or presentation
- using SoulWire branding for scams, malware, harassment, exploitation, or deceptive products

## Plugins and Integrations

Third-party plugins and integrations may identify themselves factually, but they must:

- remain clearly third-party
- avoid using SoulWire logos or Guardian identities as their own brand
- avoid suggesting approval unless they actually have it
- avoid bypassing SoulWire safety, privacy, or trust systems

## Enforcement

xxTaffyx reserves the right to:

- ask for removal or correction
- deny or revoke permission
- request renaming or rebranding
- issue copyright or trademark complaints
- pursue other enforcement steps when necessary
